How to Play

Use the two most recent radar contacts to estimate where the UFO will be on the next scan. Set the predicted bearing, then choose Intercept. Bearings are measured clockwise from north. The UFO changes bearing by the same amount each scan.

An estimate within 5° deflects the UFO. Each miss advances the simulation by 30 seconds. Choose Wait to let the UFO descend and see if it drifts clear of the base.
